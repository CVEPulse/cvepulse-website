Patch Prioritization Gap Assessment

Most VM programs don't have a scanning problem — they have a prioritization problem. CISA's BOD 26-04 just made the fix official: four risk variables, graduated deadlines, CVSS no longer required. This assessment maps your program against that model and hands you the tier design, the tooling mapping, and the executive story.

How it works

Week 1

Evidence in

You share your current SLA table, one recent scan export (Qualys/Tenable/Rapid7 CSV), and your exception or risk-acceptance process. A one-hour working session covers the parts documents don't show.

Your effort: ~2 hours
Week 2

Gap register + tier design

You receive a written gap register against the four variables, plus a graduated remediation-tier matrix adapted to your estate — sourcing each variable from tooling you already own.

Deliverable: assessment report
Week 3

Executive readout

A leadership briefing pack and a live walkthrough call: what changes, why the flat model is retired, and the efficiency case — a smaller urgent core, a defensible deferral policy.

Deliverable: briefing deck + call

Is this for you?

Good fit

  • VM lead, security manager, or CISO with a backlog that outruns your team
  • SLA model still keyed to CVSS severity bands
  • Scanner in place (Qualys, Tenable, Rapid7 or similar) but drowning in its output
  • Regulated environment — pharma, healthcare, finance — where "just auto-patch everything" isn't an option
  • Leadership asking "are we ready for AI-speed exploitation?" and you need a defensible answer

Not a fit

  • You need a scanner deployed from scratch — buy the scanner first, then come back
  • You want a 24/7 managed SOC — that's a staffed service, not a consulting engagement
  • You're looking for a compliance checkbox letter without changing anything
