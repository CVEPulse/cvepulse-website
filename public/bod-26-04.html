CISA Binding Operational Directive 26-04 · Issued June 10, 2026

The 14-day KEV deadline era is over. Four variables now decide when you patch.

BOD 26-04 replaced flat, CVSS-driven patching with a graduated risk model: exposure, known exploitation, exploit automatability, and technical impact. The highest-risk combination means 3 calendar days with mandatory forensic triage — while most of your backlog can be safely deferred. If your SOP still says "Critical = 14 days," you're now prioritizing by a retired standard.
